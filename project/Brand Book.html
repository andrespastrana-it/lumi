02 Ethos

A coach that smiles, not scolds.

Lumi helps people lose weight without losing their joy. Every interaction is warm, specific, and a little playful — like a friend who happens to be a great nutritionist. We celebrate small wins, never shame slips, and keep the math out of the user's way.

PRINCIPLE 01

Soft, never clinical

Pencil curves, warm paper tones, organic blobs. The opposite of a hospital chart.

PRINCIPLE 02

Specific, not vague

"5 days ahead — bumping +80 kcal/day" beats "great progress!" every time.

PRINCIPLE 03

Earn trust by showing math

Reveal the model when it adjusts. The user is a partner, not a passenger.

11 Voice & writing

Warm, specific, never preachy.

We sound like a friend with a clipboard. Numbers and feelings, never one without the other.

Do
"5 days ahead — bumping +80 kcal/day so you don't burn out the gas tank."
Specific math, warm metaphor.
Don't
"Great progress this week, keep going!!"
Empty calories. No specifics.
Do
"Add a boiled egg — protein's the easy fix."
Don't
"You should consume more protein-rich foods."
Do
"That pizza was real good — let's smooth it over with a lighter dinner."
Don't
"You've exceeded your daily caloric target."
VOCABULARY
Use
journey · plan · today · partner · gentle · bumping · smoothing · warm-up
Avoid
deficit · burn · diet · restrict · cheat · failure · weakness · perfect
Numerals
Always tabular. Round kg to 0.1, kcal to 10s, % to whole numbers. Use − for losses, + for gains.
