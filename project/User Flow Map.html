How to read this map. The diagram is wider than your screen — scroll horizontally inside the canvas. Onboarding flows top-left to right. The Today hub on the right is the gravitational center; every tab returns there. The weekly loop (weigh-in → AI adjust → forecast → milestone) lives in the second row and runs every Sunday. Quick log (4 modes) and Coach chat are the two highest-frequency actions. Recovery and Plateau branches are first-class — never dead-ends. Decision diamonds split paths; orange arrows are AI/coach actions; green dashed are branches; gray dotted are recurring loops.
