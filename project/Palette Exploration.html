Apricot is the hero — warm, alive, generous. It signals daylight, fruit, energy. Paired with cream as the soft fill and eucalyptus as the grounding "vegetables and progress" green, the whole palette feels like a healthy breakfast on a Saturday.

My pick — 02 · Apricot Sun

It's the rare palette that handles every emotional beat in this app. Apricot is genuinely celebratory on weigh-in wins, but the cream and paper neutrals carry 85% of the surface area — so day-to-day screens feel calm, not overstimulating. Eucalyptus does the supporting "you're on track, you're well" job without the cliché of fitness-app green.

Type pairing: Instrument Serif's gentle high contrast makes the big weight number feel intentional and human — not a stock-ticker readout. Geist below it is clean, has excellent tabular numerics for macros, and never feels corporate.

Trade-offs

  1. Sage Garden is the safer choice — would land well, but feels closer to existing wellness app conventions.
  2. Quiet Plum is gorgeous but emotionally introverted. Better for a journaling product than a daily coach.
  3. Coastal Slate wins on data clarity but loses on warmth — Marco will feel coached by a spreadsheet.
  4. Citrus & Clay is the most fun but the hardest to make calm at density. One screen too many cards and it goes loud.
